    How to Play

    You are an adventurer at the mouth of an unmapped dungeon. There is no correct path and no walkthrough — describe what you want to do in plain English and the Dungeon Master decides what happens.

    Commands that always work

    • look — survey the room again
    • go north — or south, east, west, up, down
    • take the rusted lantern — or take all
    • attack — strike whatever is trying to eat you
    • inventory — list what you carry
    • rest — recover health, if the room is safe
    • search the altar — dig for secrets and hidden loot

    You can also just… say things

    read the inscription on the arch · throw the torch into the pit · bar the door and listen · threaten the goblin with my sword

    Survival rules

    • You have 20 health. Damage is real and permanent for this run.
    • Loot the DM mentions is added to your inventory automatically — check the sidebar.
    • Enemies act on their own turn. Attacking something stronger than you is a choice you are allowed to make.
    • Score climbs with kills, treasures and discoveries.
    • At 0 health you die, and the run is over. New Dungeon starts fresh.

    If the Dungeon Master goes quiet

    The badge in the header reads LIVE DM while Groq is answering. If the network or your API key fails, it flips to LITE DM and the game keeps playing with a local fallback narrator, so you are never stuck. Reload once the connection is restored.
